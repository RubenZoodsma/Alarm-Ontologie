Situational awareness over time

The same simulation op_inference.py writes to kg_inference.trig, stepped one state change at a time. Each frame is the situational graph at that instant — alarms appearing, conditions merging onto the shared patient and device, operation state persisting after an alarm ends, then decaying to nothing. Situational shows exactly what gets exported; All entailed shows everything the reasoner actually derived at that instant, including background/taxonomic facts (e.g. an entailed superclass) the export deliberately leaves out.
